# Optimization Guidance

English | [简体中文](README_zh.md)

Use research guidance and technical knowledge to understand the current system, develop your own designs, and learn from experiments.

- [Meta-knowledge](meta-knowledge/README.md): finding references, diagnosing bottlenecks, choosing research directions, designing improvements, and recording lessons.
- [Technical knowledge](knowledge/README.md): self-contained explanations of observed behavior, mechanisms, designs, implementations, and effects, with conditions and evidence.

Use the knowledge index to select topics relevant to the current question. Entries support understanding and comparison rather than prescribe an optimization direction. Use new findings to improve them. Commands and experiment artifacts are covered by the [experiment records guide](../experiment-records.md).
