Refer to this template when writing a technique note under `guidance/`.

## Idea

Explain the problem, the concrete technique, and why it could help. Use an execution sequence, equations, or pseudocode to make the mechanism clear.

## Applicable scenarios

Describe the models, workloads, or hardware conditions where the technique is worth trying. Distinguish transferable ideas from implementation-specific requirements.

## Implementation pointers

Explain where to start, what needs to change, and the key trade-offs or unresolved questions. Link reusable code. When an existing implementation affects the value of the work, briefly identify what remains missing in the target environment.

## References

Cite papers, code, technical articles, or previous experiments. For an experiment, link its notes and relevant run records, and state the conditions and findings.
